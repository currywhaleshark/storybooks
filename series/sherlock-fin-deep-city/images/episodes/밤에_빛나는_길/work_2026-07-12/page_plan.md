## Global Continuity Locks

- Night remains cozy, not dark or frightening: deep navy/violet plus warm windows, bubble lamps, and soft gold glow.
- The winding trail stays on the alley floor and is made of many tiny gold plankton lights surrounding distinct soft green moss flakes.
- The grandfather is not clearly revealed before page `08`; cover `00` uses only a tiny distant long-legged silhouette.
- The grandfather's default face uses warm narrow crescent squints behind round glasses; avoid large round sparkling baby-like eyes.
- Only page `09`, when he first sees the glowing trail, may open his eyes slightly. Even then they remain modest, calm, and less round than a child character's eyes.
- The grandfather's lower face reads unmistakably as an elderly male grandfather. A paired set of continuous nasolabial/팔자 folds begins beside the nose under the glasses and curves down to the mouth corners; these are not short smile lines outside the mouth.
- Preserve mild soft elderly cheek heaviness around the folds across all visible face views, while keeping him healthy, warm, child-safe, and never frail or frightening.
- Page `08` is the first clear rear reveal; page `09` turns him around; page `10` proves the mechanism; page `11` shows the shared walk.
- Only the grandfather leaves moss flakes and causes the single trail. Sherlock Fin, Pearly, Pop Pop, Momo, and Crabson never create separate glowing tracks.
- Keep every named character locked to the official sheet; do not infer appearance from script prose.
- No unrelated object or character from prior episodes.
