## Shared Reference Rules

- Bright, warm children's picture-book style matching the Sherlock Fin official sheets.
- Deep City: cozy underwater jazz city, coral buildings, shell doors, bubble lights, gentle neon, no horror.
- Rounded cute silhouettes, clear big eyes, readable emotions.
- Clean reference-sheet composition on a simple light neutral or softly underwater backdrop.
- No story-page text, no labels, no random signage, no pseudo-writing, no watermark.
- Avoid photorealism, glossy 3D toy rendering, scary deep-sea realism, teeth, slime, or threatening poses.

```text
No text, no labels, no letters, no watermark.
```
