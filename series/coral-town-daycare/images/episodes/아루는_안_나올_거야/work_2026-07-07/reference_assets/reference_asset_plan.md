- A shallow, safe, toddler-scale shell-shaped water tub used inside Coral Town Daycare's warm water-play/washing space.
- Warm clear water, soft bubbles, rounded edges, no deep pool feeling.
- Pastel coral, cream, seafoam, and warm sand colors.
- Water should look clean, bright, and safe.
- Include one main front/three-quarter view and one small side/angle inset in the same reference sheet if possible.
- No characters.

### QA Checklist

- The tub reads as shallow, safe, warm, and toddler-scale.

- It has no text or pseudo-writing.
- It is simple enough to reuse across cover and pages 05-11.
- It matches the Coral Town Daycare palette and soft paper texture.
