Prompt:

Create the cover page of the Korean toddler storybook `토리야, 한 걸음만` in the Coral Town Daycare series. A5 portrait page proportion, about 1:1.414. Use the official reference images as visual truth.

Scene: a bright, warm Coral Town Daycare playground during outside playtime. A rounded pastel coral tunnel is the main playground feature, toddler-safe and soft, with thick rounded rims and warm light through the openings. The tunnel is small and consistent: just large enough that Tori would need to lower his head/body slightly to pass through, never a huge cave. 토리야, 한 걸음만

내디뎌 봐요 —

Composition: Tori and Banguli face each other across the tunnel; tunnel is fully visible; leave clean title space at the top and subtitle space near the bottom. Warm pastel watercolor and colored-pencil paper texture.
