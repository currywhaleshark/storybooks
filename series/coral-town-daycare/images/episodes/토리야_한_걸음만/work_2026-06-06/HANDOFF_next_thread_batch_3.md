## Critical Lessons From Batches 1-2

- Straight tunnel geometry is the recurring risk. Keep the coral tunnel as one straight toddler crawl-through tube:
  - no L shape
  - no corner bend
  - no side branch
  - no two separate tunnels
  - no two same-facing openings
- If both ends are visible, they must read as opposite ends of one tube. If not needed, hide or occlude the far exit.
- For page 7, Tori must not be fully inside yet. Shell/body outside, head/neck only slightly inside.
- For page 8, this is relief/discovery, not fear. The tunnel interior should be warm, pretty, and safe.
- For page 9, Tori is entering step by step; do not show the success/exit moment yet.
- Tori remains cautious but respected. No shaming, pulling, pushing, dragging, or scolding.
- Outdoor play continues to mean no child bags/straps/satchels. Keep Tori's shell and outfit, but remove bag/strap details.
- Tori scale must stay consistent. Do not make the crawling/bending Tori smaller than standing Tori.
- Banguli must stay a soft pale sky-blue water droplet mascot, not a jellyfish/glass/crystal/hard plastic object.
