## Batch 2 Locks

- A5 portrait page proportion, about `1:1.414`.
- Generate illustration and exact Korean story text together on the first pass if text remains stable.
- If text drifts, keep the best visual candidate and add deterministic text later.
- Outdoor-play rule continues: no child character wears or carries a bag. Remove child bags/satchels only; do not change species, clothing, hats, collars, scarves, fins, shells, tentacles, or core proportions.
- Tori: green turtle child, yellow hat, shell, sailor-style outfit, no bag/strap, cautious but respected.
- Lulu: coral-pink seahorse child, long snout, curled tail, pink sailor outfit/skirt feeling, coral hair decoration, no bag. Do not mix with Sua.
- Mongle: purple octopus child, eight tentacles, yellow beret, sailor collar, no bag.
- Mari teacher: purple mermaid tail, yellow apron, star hairpin, purple attendance notebook if visible.
- Banguli: soft pale sky-blue transparent water droplet mascot, not jellyfish, glass, crystal, or hard plastic.
- Coral tunnel: warm, safe, pastel coral playground tunnel. Not scary, not a dark cave, not a giant mound.
- Tunnel geometry: keep it as a straight toddler crawl-through tube. For pages where the exit is not narratively needed, one visible entrance is enough; hide or occlude the far exit to avoid confusing same-facing openings.
- Page 6 is the exception where Banguli demonstrating the pass-through may require the opposite opening. If both ends appear, they must read as opposite ends of one straight tube, not two front-facing arches on the same side.
- No shaming, dragging, scolding, pushing, or forced participation.
- Avoid extra signage, pseudo-writing outside story text, random labels, speech bubbles outside story text, human feet/legs on sea-creature children, or unrelated prior-episode content.

## 04 Page Prompt

Output candidate: `04_candidate_20260607_v1.png`

Create page 4 of the Korean toddler storybook `토리야, 한 걸음만`. A5 portrait page proportion, about 1:1.414. Use the official references as visual truth.

Scene: bright Coral Town Daycare playground. Lulu and Mongle gently invite Tori to join the tunnel play. Tori, the green turtle child with yellow hat and shell, has no bag. Tori gathers both front flippers/hands at the chest, shakes the head softly, and takes one small step backward. Tori is cautious, not ashamed or forced. Lulu, the coral-pink seahorse child, leans forward with a kind inviting gesture. Mongle, the purple octopus child with yellow beret and eight tentacles, smiles and waits. They do not pull, push, shame, or crowd Tori. Mari teacher is farther away, noticing the moment and beginning to approach. The coral tunnel is visible in the playground as a warm straight toddler tunnel, but it does not dominate the page; one visible entrance is enough.

Exact Korean story text:

```text
"토리야,
우리랑 같이 가자!"

루루가 손을 내밀었어요.
몽글이도 방긋 웃었어요.

하지만 토리는
살래살래.

"나는…… 안 할래.
그냥 여기서 볼래."

토리는 뒤로
한 걸음 물러났어요.

"그래, 괜찮아."

친구들은
기다려 줬어요.
```

Composition: middle-distance view showing space between Tori and the inviting friends. Text panel on one side. Warm, respectful mood.

## 05 Page Prompt

Output candidate: `05_candidate_20260607_v1.png`

Create page 5 of the Korean toddler storybook `토리야, 한 걸음만`. A5 portrait page proportion, about 1:1.414. Use the official references as visual truth.

Scene: Mari teacher comes close to Tori and lowers herself to Tori's eye level. She is a warm mermaid teacher with purple tail, yellow apron, star hairpin, and calm expression. She does not scold or ask "why won't you go?" Tori has no bag, gathers both front flippers/hands, and looks up at her with a slightly relieved expression. Banguli floats nearby as a soft pale sky-blue water droplet mascot with two or three tiny droplets. The coral tunnel is in the soft background, warm and safe, not scary.

Exact Korean story text:

```text
마리 선생님이
토리 옆에 앉았어요.

"토리야,
터널이 조금
무섭게 보이는구나."

토리는 끄덕였어요.

"무서운 마음은
하나도 이상하지 않아.

누구나
그럴 수 있어."

토리의 어깨가
조금 풀렸어요.
```

Composition: close middle shot. Mari teacher and Tori share eye level. Text panel on one side. Emotional focus is validation and relief.

## 06 Page Prompt

Output candidate: `06_candidate_20260607_v1.png`

Create page 6 of the Korean toddler storybook `토리야, 한 걸음만`. A5 portrait page proportion, about 1:1.414. Use the official references as visual truth.

Scene: Mari teacher gently suggests a small step, and Banguli demonstrates the tunnel first. Tori has no bag and stands near the tunnel entrance, both front flippers/hands gathered. Banguli, the soft pale sky-blue transparent water droplet mascot, moves through the tunnel and peeks out safely, winking as if to say it is okay. Tori watches with round surprised eyes and begins to take a tiny step toward the entrance. Mari teacher remains nearby, calm and encouraging. The tunnel is a warm straight toddler crawl-through tube. If both entrance and exit are shown for Banguli's demonstration, they must be opposite ends of one straight tube; do not draw two same-facing arches or an L-shaped tunnel.

Exact Korean story text:

```text
"한 번에 다
안 해도 돼.

입구까지만
가 볼까?"

그때 방울이가
터널 속으로 쏙—

그리고 반대편에서
빼꼼!

'봐, 괜찮아!'

토리는 눈을
동그랗게 떴어요.

"어? 방울이는
괜찮네……?"
```

Composition: show Banguli's safe demonstration and Tori's first tiny step. Text panel on one side. Warm, encouraging, no pressure.
