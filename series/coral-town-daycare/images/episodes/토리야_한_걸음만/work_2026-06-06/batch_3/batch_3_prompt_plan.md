## Batch 3 Locks

- A5 portrait page proportion, about `1:1.414`.
- Generate illustration and exact Korean story text together on the first pass if text remains stable.
- If text drifts, keep the best visual candidate and add deterministic text later.
- Outdoor-play rule continues: Tori has no bag, strap, satchel, or school bag. Keep Tori's shell, yellow hat, sailor outfit, species, and proportions.
- Tori: green turtle child, yellow hat with small flower, shell, sailor-style outfit, cautious but increasingly curious.
- Mari teacher: purple mermaid tail, yellow apron, star hairpin, calm expression. No human legs.
- Banguli: soft pale sky-blue transparent water droplet mascot, not jellyfish, glass, crystal, or hard plastic.
- Coral tunnel: warm, safe, pastel coral toddler crawl-through tube. Not scary, not a cave, not a giant mound.
- Tunnel geometry: keep it as one straight toddler crawl-through tube. Avoid L-shapes, corner bends, side branches, two separate tunnels, or two same-facing openings.
- Interior pages may be dimmer than playground pages, but they must feel warm and safe. Use soft light from the opposite opening and gentle shell/sea-glass details; never make the tunnel deep, black, scary, or cave-like.
- Tori scale must stay consistent. Do not shrink crawling or bending Tori compared with standing Tori. Pose changes only.
- Page 7 hard pose lock: Tori's shell/body stays outside the near entrance; only the head/neck is slightly inside the tunnel. Do not draw Tori fully crawling through yet.
- Page 8 hard mood lock: this is discovery and relief, not a fear page. The tunnel interior is pretty, warm, and safe.
- Page 9 hard action lock: Tori starts going in step by step, still cautious but moving. This is not the exit/success page yet; do not show Tori already out the other side.
- No shaming, dragging, scolding, pushing, or forced participation.
- Avoid extra signage, pseudo-writing outside story text, random labels, speech bubbles outside story text, human feet/legs on sea-creature characters, or unrelated prior-episode content.

## 07 Page Prompt

Output candidate: `07_candidate_20260607_v1.png`

Create page 7 of the Korean toddler storybook `토리야, 한 걸음만`. A5 portrait page proportion, about 1:1.414. Use the official references as visual truth.

Scene: bright Coral Town Daycare playground at the coral tunnel entrance. Tori, the green turtle child with yellow hat and shell, has no bag or strap. Tori slowly reaches the tunnel entrance and peeks inside with only the head/neck slightly entering the opening. Tori's shell and body remain outside the entrance. Tori is tense but curious, not forced. Mari teacher waits beside the entrance, low and calm, quietly giving permission to go slowly. Banguli floats inside the tunnel near the entrance, softly glowing and waiting. The tunnel interior is a little dim but warm and safe, with soft light and small shell details. The tunnel is one straight toddler crawl-through tube, not an L shape, not a cave, not a giant mound.

Exact Korean story text:

```text
토리는 터널 앞으로
천천히 갔어요.

그리고 머리만
살짝—

안을 들여다봤어요.

마리 선생님이
조용히 말했어요.

"천천히 봐도 돼."

토리는 가만히
안을 보았어요.
```

Composition: side or slight three-quarter view emphasizing that Tori's shell/body are outside while the head peeks in. Text panel on one side. The emotional focus is cautious first exploration.

## 09 Page Prompt

Output candidate: `09_candidate_20260607_v1.png`

Create page 9 of the Korean toddler storybook `토리야, 한 걸음만`. A5 portrait page proportion, about 1:1.414. Use the official references as visual truth.

Scene: Tori begins entering the coral tunnel step by step. Tori, the green turtle child with yellow hat and shell, has no bag or strap. Tori moves forward carefully: one small step, then another, mouth gently closed in concentration. The shell is starting to enter the tunnel, but Tori has not reached the exit yet. Banguli floats a little ahead, guiding the way with a warm expression and two or three tiny droplets. Warm light inside the tunnel softly surrounds Tori, with the opposite opening glowing farther ahead. The tunnel is one straight toddler crawl-through tube, not a cave, not an L shape, not two tunnels.

Exact Korean story text:

```text
토리는 입을
꼭 다물었어요.

그리고
한 걸음—

또 한 걸음—

등껍질이
쏙 들어갔어요.

방울이가 앞에서
길을 알려 줬어요.

'이쪽이야,
조금만 더!'

토리는 천천히
나아갔어요.
```

Composition: side or behind/inside view of Tori moving through the tunnel, with Banguli ahead and warm light in the distance. Text panel on one side. The emotional focus is careful courage and forward motion, not completed success.
