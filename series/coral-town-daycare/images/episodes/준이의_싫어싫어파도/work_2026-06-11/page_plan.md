## Character Fidelity Locks

- Jun-i must remain the official blue shark child: blue shark body, white belly, shark snout, dorsal fin, tail, small teeth, sailor outfit, and blue bag during pages 01-08. Do not make him fierce, realistic, toothy, or older.
- Mari teacher must match the official reference: human upper body, purple mermaid tail, half-up bob, star hairpin, yellow apron, name tag, and warm daycare-teacher expression. Do not make her a fantasy princess mermaid.
- Banguli is a small pale sky-blue transparent droplet mascot with a simple face and tiny companion droplets. It is not a jellyfish, crystal, hard bead, or toy.
- Aru must remain a true round orange pufferfish body with small spikes, small fins, pufferfish mouth, and sailor scarf. Never add human hands, feet, legs, a human torso, or a separate lower body.
- Popo must remain the moon jellyfish child from `포포.png`: translucent rounded dome, subtle inner moon-jelly pattern, small sailor collar, long soft tentacles. Eyes should be hidden or barely visible unless a page explicitly calls for eye expression.
- Lulu and Sua must preserve their seahorse silhouettes, long tube snouts, curled tails, small fins, head ridges, and official clothing details.
- Bags: pages 01-08 happen during the same arrival-time entrance sequence, so Jun-i keeps his blue bag on. Page 09 is inside after arrival, so bags are stored in cubbies/hooks/shelves and not worn. Page 10 is dismissal, so bags may be worn again.

## Emotional Continuity Locks

- Page 03 does not reveal Jun-i's true reason verbally. He only says he does not want to go in.
- Page 05 is where Mari teacher helps Jun-i find the words for his underlying feeling. Jun-i himself first says, "밖에... 더 있고 싶어."
- Page 06 works because Jun-i has named what he wants; Mari teacher then offers a timed alternative using the shell hourglass.
- The shell hourglass appears in pages 06-08 and must match `shell_hourglass_ref.png`.
- The "싫어싫어 파도" is symbolic and soft, not dangerous water. It should read as Jun-i's big feeling, not an actual disaster.
