# 준이의 싫어싫어파도 - Reference Asset Plan

## Target Asset

`series/coral-town-daycare/images/episodes/준이의_싫어싫어파도/work_2026-06-11/reference_assets/shell_hourglass_ref.png`

## Purpose

Create one locked prop reference for the shell hourglass used on pages 6-8. The earlier version had the hourglass design drifting between pages, so this asset must become the visual truth for every later prompt involving the prop.

## Visual Locks

- Small preschool-safe shell hourglass, sized for Mari teacher to hold and for Jun-i to look at closely.
- Two rounded cream-pink scallop shells form the top and bottom caps.
- Clear rounded hourglass body between the shells, soft watercolor transparency, not hard glass or crystal.
- Warm sand-colored grains inside, with a small stream of sand falling through the center.
- Tiny pearl-like round feet or soft coral nubs may support the bottom shell.
- Palette: cream, blush pink, pale coral, warm sand, a little sea-glass blue shadow.
- Style: Sanho Village Daycare soft watercolor and colored-pencil storybook prop, gentle paper texture.
- No text, no numbers, no clock face, no labels, no magical glow.
- No sharp edges, no metal frame, no jewel-like shine, no plastic toy sheen.
- Keep the prop centered with generous padding so it can be reused as a reference crop.

## QA Checklist

- The object is clearly a shell hourglass, not a lamp, trophy, snow globe, perfume bottle, or magical artifact.
- Top and bottom shells match each other and can stay consistent across pages.
- The falling sand is visible.
- The silhouette remains readable when the prop is small.
- No text or pseudo-text appears anywhere.
- The style matches the soft Sanho Village Daycare picture-book look.
