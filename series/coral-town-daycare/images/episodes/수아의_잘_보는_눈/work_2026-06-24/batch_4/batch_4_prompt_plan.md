### Output Target

### Scene Summary

At going-home time, Sua stands in front of the classroom mirror again. Unlike the earlier sad mirror moment, she now smiles brightly at herself. Her slender purple body and small eyes no longer feel dull. Banguli smiles beside the mirror. The ending is bright, warm, and self-accepting.

### References To Load

- Classroom background
- Sua no-bag
- Banguli

### Key Locks

- Sua is the central character, smiling warmly at her reflection.
- Show a round or softly framed classroom mirror with Sua's happy reflection visible.
- Sua keeps official small black button eyes; they may sparkle gently but must not become oversized glossy eyes.
- The mirror reflection should also look happy and loving, not sad.
- Banguli floats beside the mirror, smiling, with two or three small droplets.
- Use a warm classroom background, not the outdoor yard.
- Keep the entire page bright and concluding, with a large clean upper text area.
- No bags, no Lulu hairpin, no unrelated props, no sad expression.
