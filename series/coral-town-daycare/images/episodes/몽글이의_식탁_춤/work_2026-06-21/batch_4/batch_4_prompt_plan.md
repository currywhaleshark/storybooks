- No-bag indoor character references for visible children and Mari.
- Fixed seating map: `work_2026-06-21/batch_1/batch_1_fixed_seating_map_v1.png`

Primary request:
Create page 10 as the meal-finished transition scene. Preserve the dining-room continuity from page 09 v8: same round table, same camera, same practical seating, Mongle seated at the front-left stool/chair rather than on the tabletop. Mongle has finished eating and looks proud at an empty shell plate and bowl. Mari teacher warmly says that friends who finished can go out to the yard. Friends begin to rise or turn toward the exit. Banguli floats excitedly near Mongle.

Scene details:
- Empty clean plate and bowl in front of Mongle.
- Mongle's eight tentacles start to wiggle again with excitement, but below/near the chair, not across the food.
- The feeling is "we finished eating; now we can go play."
- No cleaning, no cloths, no towels, no wiping, no spill.

- Character references: Mongle, Mari, Banguli, Jun-i, Aru, Lulu, Sua, Tori, Popo.

Primary request:
Create the warm ending in the bright yard. Mongle smiles proudly and calmly after dancing, surrounded by friends. Mari teacher looks pleased. Banguli floats beside Mongle. The feeling is settled and happy: Mongle understands that during meals the legs gather, and during playtime the legs can be lively.

Scene details:

- Leave generous text space, preferably upper or side area, without covering faces.
